SOLMAR
Streets of the Sun — an open-world crime story

Solmar: sun, sea and money that never sleeps. You are Dario Voss, a wheelman who burned his last crew. Your sister Mara runs a garage on Coral Row and your old friend Kito has a plan to take the harbour back from the Vane family. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar Police, take jobs, and build a name — one mission at a time.

W A S D move / drive  ·  Mouse look (drag)  ·  Shift sprint
E enter / steal a car  ·  Space handbrake  ·  Click shoot (on foot)
R reload  ·  C camera  ·  H horn  ·  P / Esc pause
M music on/off  ·  Follow the yellow marker on the map. Stealing a car in front of police raises your wanted level — lose the cops to cool down. Your progress saves automatically.
A completely original game. No characters, names, maps or assets from any commercial game. Built with Three.js. Runs fully offline.
